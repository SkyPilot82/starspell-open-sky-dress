STARSPELL · DESIGN REVIEW · 2026-09-10

THE OPEN SKY, DRESSED

The unsigned card is the one plate in the sign deck nobody painted — and BEGIN THE CLIMB is the one label on the sheet that never learned to shine. Three dresses for the card, three voices for the letters, and the question of whether the card itself should be the button.

Skylar's ask, verbatim (9/10, ~15:10, voice): "Design pass on 'The Open Sky' needs to look prettier. 'Begin the climb' letters should shine more or remove and just click on the card."

How to rule: tap a chip on each of the three numbered questions (they sit in their sections), add notes, then COPY VERDICTS in the bottom bar and paste back to the console. Combinations are welcome in the notes — the aurora can hang over either of the other two dresses.

I · THE CARD TODAY, AND WHY IT READS PLAIN

The mock at left deals the exact sky the game deals — the living field's 22 breathing stars and 3 slow sparks sit at their true seeded places (the deck re-deals the identical sky every turn), and every 8–15 seconds a shooting star crosses, as it does on the phone. What's under them is the problem:

  • The wash is a stretched thumbnail. The night behind the stars was baked at 160×240 in v0.23, when sign cards were small art in a framed window. Since v0.79 went full-bleed it serves stretched to 404×706 — about 2.5× blown up — so its 46 baked speckles land soft while the field's sprites above them stay crisp.
  • Its neighbors are paintings. All twelve signs ship real 512×768 plates. The unsigned card is the only one still wearing the placeholder, and it's the FIRST card every fresh deck opens on (slot 0 kept, v0.95).
  • What already works stays. The v0.84 living field, the reading scrims, the crown name, the power line and the HARD box are law in every direction below — the dress goes UNDER them.

II · THREE DRESSES

Each is mocked on the real card geometry (404×706, rim, scrims, crown and foot exactly where the code puts them), with the living field dealt on top from its true seed. Every dress replaces only the stretched wash — one baked canvas, drawn once at first sheet-open and cached, the way the wash itself is. The shared placeholder texture is never touched: a sign card whose painting fails to load still borrows the old wash, byte-identical (the aries-fallback law sign-check pins).

D1 · THE WHOLE HUNT
Every constellation of the wheel, faint at once — the one card that holds everything the twelve are each a piece of. Real charts: all twelve asterisms are drawn from the shipped star data (the five beast-borrowed signs included), nameless like the campaign chart before a beast falls.
Costs: one 'opensky' bake at capped R2 ≈ 4.6 MB (≈3 painted plates; full-R knob ≈9 MB) · bake ~15 ms once · zero new sprites, Graphics, files, or motion · census pins hold (3 Graphics · 22 dots · 3 sparks).
D2 · THE MEADOW
The home meadow's own horizon rises at the card's foot — hills, grass filaments, the dark ridge grown real — under a deeper night. The classic climb begins where every climb begins: the place you're already standing. Fireflies deliberately left out: the pinned field IS the card's motion.
Costs: same single-bake path and price as D1 (≈4.6 MB at capped R2) · zero sprites/Graphics/files · census pins hold · the silhouette recipe is the meadow's own hill math, re-run at bake time.
D3 · THE AURORA VEIL
A slow aurora graded in the house palette (the water-teal, the wash's own violet, one gold whisper) hanging high on the card, breathing on a ~14 s cycle. Pretty even stilled — ?twinkle=0 and reduced motion rest it at its base alpha, the living-field seam it inherits.
Costs: one soft half-res veil bake ≈ 2 MB · +1 Image sprite on the card (its own texture key — the dot/spark counts the suite pins are untouched) · +1 tween in the layer's ledger · zero Graphics · layers over D1 or D2 for the price of itself.
Laws all three keep (the reason none of them frightens the suites): display order stays wash → dress → living field → reading scrims (v0.84's order law); the shared 'zodsky' texture is never re-baked; nothing is tinted (bakes, not tints — the Canvas shim never enters); the open-sky card's pinned census — exactly 3 Graphics, 22 dots, 3 sparks — survives every direction, so sign-check (129) needs zero edits, and would only gain a pin naming the new texture. signlevel 86 · hard 108 · endless 95 stay zero-edit.

III · BEGIN, OR THE CARD ITSELF

The truth about today's button: BEGIN THE CLIMB is u16 letters in flat parchment ink (#f4e6bd) on the painted gold-braid button — the one label in the sheet's frame with none of the wordmark's gold. Meanwhile the sheet's headline died in v0.79, which makes BEGIN the only words left that say this screen starts a climb. That fact shapes all three answers.

B · THE BUTTON GOES — the card is the button

The input truth that makes B cheap to build: the card's whole face is already one interactive swipe zone, and a press-and-release on it with no drag already happens — today it does nothing. B is one UP-handler on that standing zone. The map at right is the sheet's real tap anatomy at 420×800 design units, from the shipped container order (display order IS input order — the topOnly law):

Why a swipe can never false-fire a begin: begin rides the UP under the same 8-unit travel law that already guards ✕ (v0.79) and the HARD box (v0.70) — two shipped controls that sit ON this same swipe surface and have never mis-fired against it. A swipe that dies short of the turn threshold is a settle, not a tap: 8u–134u of travel releases into the spring-back with nothing fired. The `moving` guard also refuses taps while the deck is mid-turn, exactly as BEGIN refuses them today.

What B honestly costs:
1 · The endless door begins at once. BEGIN on the endless picker doesn't open a chart — it pins the sign and RISES. Under B, one idle tap on a pretty card starts a real climb (the campaign door only opens the map — one ✕ undoes it). This is B's sharpest edge and it cannot be dulled without a confirm sheet nobody wants.
2 · The sheet loses its only words. With the v0.79 headline gone and the button gone, a first-time player faces a wordless gallery. The mock ships the compromise: a whisper — "tap your card to begin" — in the freed band (+5 strings, one per language).
3 · The first sister-suite edits since v0.70. hard-check (341, 514), endless-check and sign-check all enter climbs by tapping the braid button they find by texture; B rewrites those sites to card-taps and adds the no-false-fire checks (settle-release ≠ begin, island taps ≠ begin). Priced in §IV — it's real work, not a law broken; the zero-edit record just ends.

C · BOTH — the shined button anchors, the card answers too

The button stays exactly where it stands, wearing A's gold (shown at A2; A3 adds the strip's glimmer). The card-tap rides beside it under B's full rule set — same 8-unit law, same flick guard, same `moving` refusal. The catch band under the button stays: with a button present, near-misses still deserve absorbing.

Suite bill: identical to B's card-tap checks (the new begin path needs proving) — but nothing is rewritten, only added: every harness that taps the braid keeps working untouched, because the braid is still there.

★ RECOMMENDED: C, with the stamp at A2 or A3.
With the headline gone since v0.79, BEGIN is the only phrase left that tells a first-timer this sheet starts a climb — so the teacher stays, dressed at last in the wordmark's own gold, which is everything the "shine" ask actually wants. And the thumb that taps the plate itself gets answered instead of ignored: the tap costs one UP-handler on a zone that already exists, guarded by the same 8-unit law that has kept ✕ and HARD honest on this exact surface since v0.70.

IV · WHAT BUILDING IT WOULD MEAN

Three separate cards, each firing only on its own "done and agreed":
1 · the dress — one 'opensky' canvas baked from shipped chart data at first sheet-open (capped R2 ≈4.6 MB; full-R knob ≈9 MB named, not assumed), display order wash→dress→field→scrims, 'zodsky' untouched (aries-fallback byte-identical), sign-check gains a texture pin — ~0.5–1 day including the DPR-3 on-device look (mocks here are SVG approximations; the bake is judged on a phone, the taurus-blend precedent).
2 · the shine — the ssGoldTex stamp keyed per label ×5 languages with the de scale guard; A3's glimmer = one masked highlight Image + one ledgered tween, stilled by ?twinkle=0 / reduced motion — ~0.5 day, zero suite edits.
3 · the card-tap (B or C's tap) — one UP-handler on the standing swipe zone (8u travel + flick + moving guards), C keeps button and catch band, B also retires both and ships the 5-string whisper — ~0.5 day + ~0.5 day of harness: sign-check begin sites, hard-check 341/514, endless-check entries (rewritten under B, merely joined under C) plus the new no-false-fire checks. The first sister-suite edits since v0.70, priced here and chosen knowingly or not at all.

Named honestly: the full-card bake is the game's biggest texture in any direction (the knob halves it); an idle tap on the endless door under B/C starts a real climb (C keeps the button as the honest target, which is half the argument for it); the freed 90 units under B are NOT spent here — growing the card is its own geometry card; nothing in any direction touches the wire, the profile, or a single byte outside game.js/strings.js and the suites.

Nothing here is built. This page is a look, not a queue item — each card fires only on "done and agreed", separately.
